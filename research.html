Research Journal

AllSpark studies foundation models and agents in real-world interaction systems. Three interlocking areas form our research program.

Our Approach →
Foundation Models
Advance base models that learn, reason, and generalize across modalities and environments. We study how real-world interaction shapes fundamental capabilities — from language understanding to multimodal grounding and long-context reasoning.
Pretraining Scaling Capabilities
8 essays →
Agentic Systems
Build agents that perceive, plan, act, and learn in complex, real-world interaction loops. We design agentic runtimes, study tool-use failure modes, and develop methods for agents to improve from their own trajectories.
Planning Tool Use Memory Reflection
7 essays →
Evaluation & Harness
Design rigorous evaluations and reusable harnesses to measure capability and behavior. We treat evaluation as infrastructure — not a post-hoc check, but the instrument that defines what progress means.
