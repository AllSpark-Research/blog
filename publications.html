Vela: User Behavior as Supervision Sparks LLM Intelligence
Turns anonymized real user behavior records into trainable tasks and trajectories, showing that long-term behavior data is a scalable supervision source for both user-centered and general LLM capabilities.

AdaptEvo: Learning under Imperfect Supervision through Confidence-Adaptive Training with Knowledge Evolution
Couples Confidence-Adaptive GRPO with evolving decision knowledge and process rubrics, so models learn from imperfect supervision and stay robust on rule-governed multimodal moderation even after the rules change.

Preprint Sep 2026 arXiv:2609.35954
ROSS: Relearning from Self-Generated Rollouts through Selective Supervision
Keeps historical self-generated rollouts as context while applying loss only to selected continuations, extracting further gains from past experience without spending new policy rollouts.

Skill2Env: Capability-Oriented Environment Synthesis from Skills for General Agents
Starts from a skill and lets agent capability demands drive joint synthesis of tasks and executable environments, then raises difficulty through Iterative Task Hardening guided by solver execution evidence.

Preprint Sep 2026 arXiv:2609.26355
PACT: From Credit Assignment to Critic Alignment
Proves that Completeness, Prefix Consistency, and Neutrality uniquely determine token-level credit, and uses this lens to align critic training with the updated policy via an Actor-then-Critic order.
